# Elliott: in-game references batch

Concrete in-world facts, every one checked against `elliott-setting.md` sections 3, 9 and 11: gift
tastes and the two cucumbers he hands straight back, food and drink, the movie theater, all nine
festivals, and the small true details of a life on the beach. He has no personal quest, so none is
invented here.

- player: Thought your desk could use a refill.

- elliott: I shall use it badly for a fortnight and then well for one glorious afternoon. That is the usual ratio.

### elliott-ref-003

### elliott-ref-022

- player: Are you hunting eggs this year?
- elliott: In my own fashion. Taking breaks from work can make you more productive in the long run, and I have taken a magnificent break already.

### elliott-ref-037
context: winter, snowing morning, Elliott's cabin, 6 hearts
- player: How do you fill an entire winter in here?
- elliott: Badly and thoroughly. I do little indoor exercises since it is too cold to go out, I scrub the algae off the floorboards, and I brush this hair daily or it clumps into messy knots.
- player: This place has spiders, you know.
- elliott: It does, and they stay unsquashed. If you find one, tell me and I shall carry the poor thing outside myself.
- player: You are too soft.
- elliott: Very likely. It costs me one walk to the door and buys me a clear conscience for the afternoon.

### elliott-ref-038
context: fall, clear afternoon, the library, 7 hearts
- player: You are in here every day lately.
- elliott: From the moment it opens until half past five, all through fall and winter. There is warmth, and quiet, and shelves that never once ask me how the novel is coming along.
- player: What are you reading?
- elliott: One of the classics, to get the creative juices flowing. On a dull day I read somebody better than me until the envy turns back into ambition.
