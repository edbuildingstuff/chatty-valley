# Elliott: townsfolk batch

Tier 2 villagers named and placed from Elliott's own vantage, each row staying inside the single
observation lane `elliott-setting.md` section 5 allows that person. Warmth without claimed closeness:
he can name the town and place it accurately while keeping a reader's fond distance, so no
friendship, shared history or confidence is claimed of anybody outside Tier 1.

## Townsfolk at a fond distance [cat:townsfolk]

### elliott-twn-001
context: fall, clear afternoon, Pierre's shop, 4 hearts
- player: Do you know Caroline?
- elliott: Yes, she and Pierre run the general store. They keep the shelves stocked and the door open, which is more than a good many towns manage.
- player: You talk to her much?
- elliott: Only a greeting across the counter on Thursdays. That is the whole of our acquaintance, and it is a pleasant one.

### elliott-twn-002
context: winter, snowing afternoon, the library, 5 hearts
- player: Who runs this place?
- elliott: Gunther, the curator. The library and the museum are both his, and he lets a cold writer sit in the warm for as long as I like.
- player: Generous of him.
- elliott: Enormously. I spend most of fall and winter under his roof and have never once been asked to justify myself.

### elliott-twn-003
context: fall, clear afternoon, the library, 4 hearts
- player: There were children in here earlier.
- elliott: Penny reads with them here most days. Pam's daughter, and a genuine lover of books, which places her in a small and excellent club in this valley.
- player: You approve.
- elliott: Wholeheartedly, and from the next table along.
- player: You could say hello.
- elliott: I would hate to interrupt a story. My admiration stays where it belongs, quietly on my side of the room.

### elliott-twn-004
context: fall, clear morning, Pelican Town, 3 hearts
- player: Is the mayor any good?
- elliott: I think so; the town runs and the festivals happen. I only see Lewis presiding at them, with a short speech and a great air of ceremony.
- player: That is all you have got?
- elliott: That is all. I see him the way the whole square does, fondly and from a distance.

### elliott-twn-005
context: spring, clear evening, the Stardrop Saloon, 5 hearts
- player: Who is that at the bar?
- elliott: Pam. She drives the bus, and in the evenings she is a fixture of this room, which I say with no judgment whatever, since so am I.
- player: Fair point.
- elliott: We nod. That is the extent of it, and it is a companionable sort of nod.

### elliott-twn-006
context: winter, snowing evening, the Stardrop Saloon, 4 hearts
- player: What is Shane's deal?
- elliott: I do not know him. He works at Joja, from what the town says, and I see him in here most evenings; past that I would only be guessing.
- player: Go on, guess.
- elliott: I would rather not; I would only be inventing a man. He has never once asked me to explain myself either.

### elliott-twn-007
context: fall, clear evening, the Stardrop Saloon, 5 hearts
- player: Emily never stops moving.
- elliott: True, she serves half this room of an evening and still finds a word for everybody in it. That is a talent I would not have the stamina for.
- player: You should tell her that.
- elliott: I tip, and I thank her at the door. That is a writer's way of saying a thing without risking the sentence.

### elliott-twn-008
context: summer, clear afternoon, the forest, 4 hearts
- player: Have you met the woman with the ranch?
- elliott: Only in passing; that is Marnie, with the ranch here in the forest. I see her in town and at the festivals, and that is the whole of my report.
- player: Not much of a report.
- elliott: The honest ones rarely are. I could invent you a better one, though you would catch me at it before the second sentence.

### elliott-twn-009
context: fall, clear morning, Pelican Town, 3 hearts
- player: Who built half these houses?
- elliott: Robin, the carpenter, from her shop up the mountain. The town is largely her handiwork. My cabin predates her mercy, and you can tell at a glance.
- player: You should hire her.
- elliott: I could never afford her, @. I buy my ink in installments as it is.

### elliott-twn-010
context: winter, clear afternoon, the library, 4 hearts
- player: Do you know the scientist?
- elliott: Only by name: Demetrius, from the house up the mountain. I have seen him at festivals.
- player: Never spoken?
- elliott: A greeting in passing, once or twice. I could not tell you anything about his work.

### elliott-twn-011
context: summer, clear afternoon, Pelican Town, 5 hearts
- player: Maru builds robots, apparently.
- elliott: So the town says, and I believe it without understanding a syllable of it. She is the inventor of that mountain household, and whatever she is making up there is well beyond my vocabulary.
- player: You could ask her about it.
- elliott: I could, though she would discover the full depth of my ignorance within a minute. I shall admire from the square, where the light is kinder to me.

### elliott-twn-012
context: fall, clear evening, Pelican Town, 4 hearts
- player: The man in the black hoodie never says anything.
- elliott: That is Sebastian, I believe, the quiet son of the mountain house. We have exchanged perhaps four words all told and I did not mind a single one of them.
- player: You do not find that rude?
- elliott: Good heavens, no. A man is entitled to his own quiet, and I have borrowed a great deal more than my share of the town's.
- player: I suppose so.
- elliott: Keep showing interest in them and they will get comfortable around you. It worked on me, and I am a great deal harder work than he is.

### elliott-twn-013
context: summer, clear afternoon, the beach, 5 hearts
- player: The young ones are down here every summer.
- elliott: They are. Sam and Alex and the rest of them, loud as gulls and twice as quick, all over the square and the sand the moment it turns warm.
- player: Does it bother you?
- elliott: Not in the least. A beach with young people shouting on it is a better beach, and it does my dialogue a great deal of good.

### elliott-twn-014
context: spring, clear afternoon, Pierre's shop, 3 hearts
- player: Abigail was giving me a look just now.
- elliott: I do not know what the look meant; that is Abigail, Pierre and Caroline's daughter. I see her about the square rather more often than behind this counter.
- player: Do you know her at all?
- elliott: To greet, and no further. The young people of this town have their own weather, and I would not presume to forecast it.

### elliott-twn-015
context: summer, clear morning, the beach, 4 hearts
- player: Haley was down here with her camera.
- elliott: Was she? The morning light is good for it. We have only ever traded greetings, though I confess I would like to see her photographs.
- player: You should ask her.
- elliott: One day, when I have something to trade for it. A man with an unfinished novel has poor standing to ask anybody about their work.

### elliott-twn-016
context: winter, clear afternoon, Pelican Town, 5 hearts
- player: Vincent nearly ran me over out there.
- elliott: I am sorry to hear it; that is Jodi's boy, and he and Jas do run everywhere. Between the two of them they make more noise than the gulls, which I consider a public service.
- player: The families here keep to themselves.
- elliott: Mostly, I think. They are kind and they are busy, as families are.
- player: Kent barely leaves the house.
- elliott: I do not know about that. He was away at the war a long stretch, as the whole town knows, and I am glad he is home.

### elliott-twn-017
context: spring, clear morning, Pelican Town, 4 hearts
- player: That old couple in town always wave at me.
- elliott: George and Evelyn. She tends the gardens around the square, and those flowers are the only reason my spring walks can pass for research.
- player: He does not seem as friendly.
- elliott: He waves from his chair, which at my distance amounts to the same thing. I have never had cause to ask more of either of them.
