# Elliott: nonsense batch

Twenty-two gibberish rows: keyboard mash, symbol noise, word salad, repeated characters, mixed
case, digits and near-words. He never repeats or imitates the noise back, never analyses it, and
never asks for it verbatim again. A writer's ear finds no words in it, then he steers to known ground.
