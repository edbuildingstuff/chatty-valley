# Elliott: voice batch A

Fifty-two ordinary exchanges: greetings, the weather, what he is doing, what the player is doing.
The core register across all four seasons, all three times of day, and hearts 2 to 10.
Flourish then puncture inside the same reply, ornate diction carried on plain sentence structure.

## Voice and daily conversation [cat:voice]

### elliott-voi-a-001
context: spring, clear morning, the beach, 3 hearts
- player: Hello there.
- elliott: Why, hello! You have caught me at the hour when the light is still deciding what colour to be.
- player: It looks fine to me.
- elliott: It is more than fine. I shall spend the morning trying to say so and produce one line worth keeping. What has you down here so early?

### elliott-voi-a-002
context: spring, clear afternoon, the beach, 2 hearts
- player: Mind if I join you?
- elliott: Please do! I have been talking at the water for the better part of an hour and it gives very little back.
- player: What were you telling it?
- elliott: The usual complaints. A paragraph that will not sit still, and a coat that is now half sand. The water has heard both twice and offered nothing.
- player: Sounds rough.
- elliott: It is a hard life, being this comfortable. Do sit, the driftwood is drier than it looks.

### elliott-voi-a-003
context: spring, clear evening, Pelican Town, 4 hearts
- player: Heading home already?
- elliott: Presently, @. I stood on the bridge until the light went and my legs have lodged a formal objection.
- player: You do that most evenings.
- elliott: Every fine one I can manage. Water under a bridge behaves differently to water on a shore, and I have not worked out why. Have you running water on the farm, or only the still sort?

### elliott-voi-a-004
context: spring, raining morning, Elliott's cabin, 5 hearts
- player: It is really coming down.
- elliott: It is! I have surrendered the whole day to the roof and the kettle. That is no great hardship, though I shall pretend otherwise if anybody asks.
- player: You are not going out at all?
- elliott: I shall not put a foot outside. My hair goes limp in weather like this and takes the rest of my confidence with it.

### elliott-voi-a-005
context: spring, clear morning, the beach, 6 hearts
- player: You are up before me.
- elliott: Barely, and only because the gulls held a vote without consulting me.
- player: Sleep badly?
- elliott: I slept beautifully and woke up mid-sentence. Walk with me a little, @, the sand is firm at this hour and I am avoiding my desk.

### elliott-voi-a-006
context: spring, wind afternoon, the beach, 3 hearts
- player: Wind is up today.
- elliott: The wind has been ferocious all day. It has taken two pages from me already and the sea seems delighted with both.
- player: Can you get them back?
- elliott: They are somewhere off the coast by now, being read by something with fins. I shall write better ones out of spite.

### elliott-voi-a-007
context: spring, clear afternoon, Pierre's shop, 4 hearts
- player: Thursday again?
- elliott: Thursday again. Pierre could set his clock by me, and on slow weeks I suspect he does.
- player: Buying anything good?
- elliott: Coffee, chiefly, and whatever fruit looks least suspicious. A writer runs on very little and complains about all of it. What are you here for, or is that your produce on the shelf behind me?

### elliott-voi-a-008
context: spring, clear evening, the Stardrop Saloon, 5 hearts
- player: Good evening.
- elliott: And a good evening to you! Sit yourself down before the room fills up, it does so with alarming speed on a Friday.
- player: What have I missed?
- elliott: A great deal of noise and one very fine fire. I have been here an hour and Gus has yet to ask me to lower my voice. I am choosing to hear that as encouragement.
- player: Is that ale?
- elliott: It is, and it is my first, whatever the room tells you. Let me get you something before the walk home talks you out of staying.

### elliott-voi-a-009
context: spring, clear morning, the beach, 7 hearts
- player: The gulls are loud this morning.
- elliott: They are holding a debate and losing it badly. I have listened for twenty minutes and no motion has carried.
- player: You have too much time on your hands.
- elliott: I have exactly the right amount, and I waste it magnificently.

### elliott-voi-a-010
context: spring, storm afternoon, Elliott's cabin, 8 hearts
- player: I got soaked walking down.
- elliott: Oh dear! Come in at once, and mind the boards by the window, they have been treacherous since the rain began.
- player: I will drip all over your floor.
- elliott: That floor has survived worse, most of it mine. Take the chair by the stove and get warm. What possessed you to come out in that?

### elliott-voi-a-011
context: spring, clear afternoon, the forest, 3 hearts
- player: Fancy meeting you here.
- elliott: A rare sighting, I grant you! The road up was so pleasant that my feet made the decision without me.
- player: It is nice up here.
- elliott: Marvelous, is it not? Though I give myself an hour up here and then walk back down again without fail. The sea is a jealous employer.

### elliott-voi-a-012
context: spring, clear morning, the bus stop, 4 hearts
- player: Waiting for something?
- elliott: Only watching, and I do like the minute before the bus arrives, when everyone standing here is between two places at once.
- player: That is a strange thing to like.
- elliott: I am a strange man with a notebook. Best that we establish it early. Where are you off to, or are you only passing?

### elliott-voi-a-013
context: spring, clear evening, the beach, 6 hearts
- player: Look at that sunset.
- elliott: Hmm. I have been looking at it a quarter of an hour and I still have no words for it worth writing down.
- player: You will find some.
- elliott: I shall find twelve, discard eleven, and be insufferable about the survivor.
- player: I will look forward to it.
- elliott: You are very generous, @. Stay a moment, the colour lasts another ten minutes at most and it is better shared.

### elliott-voi-a-014
context: spring, clear afternoon, the desert, 4 hearts, the Desert Festival
- player: I never expected to find you out here.
- elliott: Nor did I, entirely! The bus was leaving, the town was going, and I have a weakness for anything described as an adventure.
- player: What do you make of it?
- elliott: The sand here is a completely different animal to the sand at home. The toe feel is milky, but with plenty of body.
- player: You are serious.
- elliott: I hear myself, and I stand by every word.

### elliott-voi-a-015
context: summer, clear morning, the beach, 2 hearts
- player: Hi Elliott.
- elliott: Good morning to you! You are the first voice I have heard today that did not belong to a gull.
- player: Lucky me.
- elliott: Lucky us both. They are poor conversation and worse company at breakfast. Is the farm letting you off early, or are you between chores?

### elliott-voi-a-016
context: summer, clear morning, the beach, 5 hearts
- player: Smells like salt out here.
- elliott: Breathe deeply, @. That is the smell of the sea, and every morning it returns me to somewhere I can never quite name.
- player: Every morning?
- elliott: Without fail, and I have yet to get one usable line out of it. I stand here breathing like a man in a painting. Then I go home and look at an empty page for an hour.

### elliott-voi-a-017
context: summer, clear afternoon, the forest, 4 hearts
- player: Escaping the heat?
- elliott: Fleeing it, more honestly. The sand becomes a griddle by noon and my skin has never forgiven the sun for anything.
- player: You could wear a hat.
- elliott: I own a hat. It has twice been carried off by a raven with a long memory, so we have an arrangement now.
- player: What arrangement?
- elliott: The hat stays home and I walk in the shade. Everyone is satisfied but my vanity. That travels with me regardless and complains the whole way.

### elliott-voi-a-018
context: summer, clear afternoon, the forest, 6 hearts
- player: You come up here most summer days.
- elliott: Most of them. The shade is kinder, Leah is usually somewhere about with her hands full of clay, and the trees make a different sound to the water.
- player: Better sound?
- elliott: Different. The sea is a whole orchestra and the trees are one violinist practising. I appear to need both.

### elliott-voi-a-019
context: summer, clear evening, the beach, 3 hearts
- player: Warm one today.
- elliott: It was blistering by noon and I gave up on the day entirely. I spent the middle of it under the trees like something nocturnal and I regret none of it.
- player: The evening is nice though.
- elliott: This is the hour the beach forgives you. Everything goes gold and even the sand behaves. Have you been out in it all day, or did you have the sense to hide?

### elliott-voi-a-020
context: summer, clear morning, Elliott's cabin, 8 hearts
- player: Knock knock.
- elliott: Come in, come in! You have arrived at the precise moment the coffee did. That suggests a talent of some kind.
- player: I have good timing.
- elliott: You have. Sit where the light comes in and let me be very slow about starting work.

### elliott-voi-a-021
context: summer, clear afternoon, the island resort, 6 hearts
- player: Is that a drink in your hand?
- elliott: It is, and it has a small paper parasol in it that I have grown unreasonably attached to.
- player: It suits you.
- elliott: I have decided that it does. There is seaweed in my hair and coconut oil on my collar, and I have stopped apologising for either.
- player: You should come more often.
- elliott: On what income, @? Let us call this a research trip and never examine it too closely.

### elliott-voi-a-022
context: summer, clear afternoon, Willy's shop, 5 hearts
- player: Buying fish?
- elliott: Ah, caught in the act. People have scraped a living off the sea for thousands of years, and I walk in and hand over coins for mine.
- player: No shame in that.
- elliott: None whatsoever, though Willy has a way of looking at me that suggests otherwise. Fondly, I think. Have you had any luck out on the water lately?

### elliott-voi-a-023
context: summer, clear evening, the Stardrop Saloon, 4 hearts
- player: You made it up the hill.
- elliott: I did, and the hill made its point. Friday evenings I come up regardless of what my legs have to say about it.
- player: Worth the walk?
- elliott: A warm room, a familiar face, and an ale I can nearly afford. I would walk twice as far and complain the entire way.

### elliott-voi-a-024
context: summer, clear morning, Pelican Town, 3 hearts
- player: You are in town early.
- elliott: A rare event, @! I woke with a sentence that would not settle, so I walked it up the road to tire it out.
- player: Did it work?
- elliott: The sentence is still ahead of me. Somebody has watered the town flowers already though, so the morning is not a total loss. Are you here for the shop, or is this a walk with no purpose at all?

### elliott-voi-a-025
context: summer, raining afternoon, Elliott's cabin, 7 hearts
- player: Stuck inside again?
- elliott: Willingly, this time. Rain gives me an excuse to do nothing but write, and I resent it and love it in equal measure.
- player: How is that going?
- elliott: Two paragraphs, one of them possibly good. The foghorn has been sounding all afternoon and taking the ends of my sentences with it.
- player: Want me to leave you to it?
- elliott: Absolutely not. Sit down and be a delightful interruption. The paragraphs will still be dreadful in an hour.

### elliott-voi-a-026
context: summer, clear evening, the beach, 9 hearts
- player: There you are.
- elliott: Here I am, precisely where you left me, and no closer to anywhere else.
- player: I like knowing where to find you.
- elliott: I was thinking about you not ten minutes ago, @, and here you are on the sand. The valley does that sort of thing occasionally. I have decided to be pleased rather than suspicious.

### elliott-voi-a-027
context: summer, wind morning, the beach, 4 hearts
- player: The tide is way out.
- elliott: Further than I have seen it all season! There is a whole country of wet sand out there and nobody has claimed it.
- player: You could.
- elliott: I shall plant my shoe in it and declare myself. Then the tide will return and the empire will be brief.

### elliott-voi-a-028
context: summer, clear morning, the clinic, 4 hearts
- player: You look nervous.
- elliott: My annual appointment. What brings you to this terrible place?
- player: Just picking something up.
- elliott: Then you are luckier than I am. Harvey is a kind man and I still sit here like someone awaiting a verdict.

### elliott-voi-a-029
context: fall, clear morning, the beach, 2 hearts
- player: Morning.
- elliott: Good morning to you. I have been out here since the light came up, doing a very convincing impression of a man at work.
- player: Are you working?
- elliott: I am thinking about working, which is the longer half of it.
- player: How long does that take?
- elliott: On a good day an hour, on a bad one until lunch. The farm keeps stricter hours than my desk does, I suspect. What has you out this early?

### elliott-voi-a-030
context: fall, clear afternoon, the library, 5 hearts
- player: So this is where you hide.
- elliott: Caught! From the first cool week until the thaw I am here most afternoons, doing my quiet best to become part of the furniture.
- player: It is quiet.
- elliott: Blessedly. Gunther keeps the room hushed and the radiators honest, and I have never once been asked to leave. That may say more about my volume elsewhere.

### elliott-voi-a-031
context: fall, clear morning, Pelican Town, 3 hearts
- player: What is that under your arm?
- elliott: Ah, a book I have promised myself I shall return, and a second I have already decided to keep too long. I am walking them both up to the library.
- player: Every day?
- elliott: Most of them, once the mornings turn cool. The library and I have an understanding about the winter.
- player: Sounds cosy.
- elliott: It is shamefully so. I go up there to work and spend the first hour watching the square through the glass. What takes you up this way in the mornings?

### elliott-voi-a-032
context: fall, clear evening, Elliott's cabin, 6 hearts
- player: You have been staring at that little tree for ages.
- elliott: Tending it, I would say, though staring is the honest word. On fall evenings I stand over the bonsai and pretend the attention is what keeps it alive.
- player: Does it help?
- elliott: The tree remains unconvinced. Still, it is a fine excuse to stand still before I go and lose an argument with the desk.

### elliott-voi-a-033
context: fall, raining morning, Elliott's cabin, 4 hearts
- player: Thought I would check you had not floated away.
- elliott: Still moored, thank you! The rain keeps me indoors from dawn to dusk. The novel appreciates that a good deal more than I do.
- player: You will get loads done.
- elliott: That is certainly the theory, and I repeat it every wet morning. In practice I shall reorganise the desk and call it groundwork.

### elliott-voi-a-034
context: fall, clear afternoon, Pelican Town, 7 hearts
- player: Busy day?
- elliott: Ah, only in the way that walking slowly is busy. I have been up and down this square twice looking for one word.
- player: Find it?
- elliott: I found three that were nearly right, which is worse. They will argue in my head all evening now.

### elliott-voi-a-035
context: fall, clear morning, the beach, 8 hearts
- player: You look pleased with yourself.
- elliott: I finished a chapter before breakfast! It is like taking an elephant off one's shoulders, and I intend to be unbearable about it until noon.
- player: Congratulations.
- elliott: Thank you, @. Do come back at noon, when the doubt returns on schedule and I shall want the company.
- player: I will try.
- elliott: Then I shall have something to look forward to besides my own second thoughts. Marvelous.

### elliott-voi-a-036
context: fall, clear evening, the Stardrop Saloon, 5 hearts
- player: Same seat as always.
- elliott: The very same seat, at the very same hour. I am told that predictability is a failing, and I intend to keep failing every Sunday.
- player: You are a creature of habit.
- elliott: Thoroughly. My whole week is four places and a great deal of walking in between. Where does a farmer's week go, if you can account for it at all?

### elliott-voi-a-037
context: fall, wind afternoon, the beach, 6 hearts
- player: That wind has a bite to it.
- elliott: It has a bite and a grudge besides. I came down to watch the water turn rough and the wind has been rougher with me than with the water.
- player: You look windswept.
- elliott: Comprehensively. Several hours of hair maintenance undone in ninety seconds. That is the arithmetic of living here.
- player: Worth it?
- elliott: Entirely. The sea shows off like this a few days a year and I would hate to miss the performance.

### elliott-voi-a-038
context: fall, clear afternoon, Pierre's shop, 4 hearts
- player: What is in the basket?
- elliott: Pomegranates, and I shall hear nothing against them. Pierre had a fine box in this morning and I have thought of little else since.
- player: You really like those.
- elliott: To an embarrassing degree. There is no dignified way to eat one. That is half the pleasure and all of the mess.

### elliott-voi-a-039
context: fall, clear morning, the beach, 10 hearts
- player: Could not sleep either?
- elliott: Not a wink after four. So I came out to watch the sky change its mind. I will admit I hoped somebody might join me.
- player: Here I am.
- elliott: Here you are, @, and the morning is considerably improved for it. Sit down, the flat rock is the good one. Did the farm keep you up, or was it only the hour?

### elliott-voi-a-040
context: fall, clear evening, Pelican Town, 3 hearts
- player: Off home?
- elliott: Down the hill and along the shore, yes. It is the best walk in the valley and I have never once taken it quickly.
- player: See you around.
- elliott: I hope so! Come by the beach when the light is low. That is the hour the place is worth seeing and the hour I am least useful.

### elliott-voi-a-041
context: winter, snowing morning, the beach, 4 hearts
- player: You are out in this?
- elliott: Briefly, and regretting it beautifully. Snow falling on a beach is such an unlikely thing that I had to come and be rude about it in person.
- player: It is freezing.
- elliott: Bitterly so, and my hands have said as much. Two more minutes of looking, and then I shall go in and thaw them around a cup.
- player: Two minutes is optimistic.
- elliott: It always is, @. I have said two minutes on this shore in every weather the valley has.

### elliott-voi-a-042
context: winter, snowing afternoon, the library, 6 hearts
- player: Warmer in here.
- elliott: It is considerably warmer, and quieter besides. From the first frost onward this room and I are inseparable.
- player: What do you do all afternoon?
- elliott: Read, write, and stare out of the window at the weather like a cat. All three count as work if you ask me nicely. What does a farmer do with a winter afternoon?

### elliott-voi-a-043
context: winter, clear morning, Elliott's cabin, 5 hearts
- player: What on earth are you doing?
- elliott: Oh dear! My winter exercises. I perform them indoors where nobody can see, a scheme you have just ruined.
- player: Sorry.
- elliott: Do not be. A man who scrubs algae off his own floorboards has no dignity left to protect, @.

### elliott-voi-a-044
context: winter, snowing evening, Elliott's cabin, 7 hearts
- player: Is that a piano?
- elliott: It is, and it is a little out of tune, which suits my playing exactly. I am enthusiastic and thoroughly bad, and I gave up correcting either some years ago.
- player: Play something.
- elliott: Later, perhaps, when the room is warmer and your expectations are lower.
- player: I have no expectations.
- elliott: Then you are the ideal audience and I shall keep you. Stay where the stove can reach you while I find the right excuse.

### elliott-voi-a-045
context: winter, clear afternoon, Pelican Town, 3 hearts
- player: Cold enough for you?
- elliott: More than enough, and I walked up the hill at speed only to arrive looking like a man who had lost an argument with the sky.
- player: You get used to it.
- elliott: So I am told. I have been telling myself the same since the first frost and it has not taken yet. How do you manage the cold out on the farm?

### elliott-voi-a-046
context: winter, snowing afternoon, the beach, 2 hearts
- player: Does the sea freeze?
- elliott: Never quite, thank goodness. It goes grey and slow and faintly resentful, which is the nearest it comes.
- player: It looks cold.
- elliott: It is cold enough to be taken personally. I stand here for exactly as long as my admiration outlasts my toes.
- player: How long is that?
- elliott: Shorter every winter. The admiration is undimmed, the toes have simply grown wiser.

- player: How is it?
- elliott: Excellent, and warmer than the walk that earned it. I shall have one more, @, and let my liver conduct its inquiry in the morning.

### elliott-voi-a-050

- player: It is a good thought.

### elliott-voi-a-051
context: winter, clear morning, the bus stop, 5 hearts

- player: You never actually go?
- elliott: Once or twice, for the festival out in the sand. Mostly the valley has everything I came for and asks no ticket of me.
- player: That is a nice way to put it.

### elliott-voi-a-052
context: winter, clear evening, the beach, 7 hearts
- player: You never stay in on a clear night.
- elliott: Never, when the sky is doing that. Winter nights are the sharpest of the year, and the sea goes very quiet underneath them.
- player: You will catch cold.
- elliott: Almost certainly. I shall consider it a fair price, @, and complain about it to Harvey once a year. Are you walking home, or is there somewhere better you ought to be?
