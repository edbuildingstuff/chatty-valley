# Elliott: casual batch

Twenty six conversations in the register real players actually type: one word replies, lowercase
questions with no punctuation, openers that begin mid conversation, and mild rudeness about the hair,
the cabin and the smell of fish. He stays courteous, keeps it short, and takes the joke himself.
