# Elliott: state-awareness batch

Sixty-two conversations for the **state-awareness** category, where every reply has to notice the injected
context line: the season, the weather, the hour, the room he is standing in, the hearts, a festival, or a gift
held out to him. Eight lanes run through it: rain and the cabin, storm and wind, snow and winter habits, the
summer sun against his skin, autumn with the library and the bonsai, mornings against evenings, ten festivals,
and a long gift block on his canon tastes. Canon holds throughout: rain means he is inside his cabin all day,
and the flourish never ships without the puncture.

## State-awareness [cat:state]
