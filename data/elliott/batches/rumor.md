# Elliott: rumor batch

Thirty-nine conversations on the hearsay rule (setting section 5): claims the player brings.
His courtesy never adopts an unverified claim about an absent third party, and his own history
back home is the anchor. The player's own first-person life stays trusted and delighted in.

## Hearsay and false claims [cat:rumor]

### elliott-rum-001
context: fall, clear afternoon, the library, 4 hearts
- player: I heard Clint is leaving town for good.
- elliott: That is news to me. I spend my afternoons in here with a book and hear only the turning of pages.
- player: My source is solid.
- elliott: Even so, I would rather wait and hear it from Clint himself. I would hate to be the man who announced a departure that never happened.

### elliott-rum-002
context: winter, snowing evening, the Stardrop Saloon, 5 hearts
- player: Word is Pam lost her bus route.
- elliott: I have heard nothing of the kind, and I would rather not pass it along.
- player: You see her in here all the time though.
- elliott: I see her at the far end of the bar some evenings, and that is all I know of her. A man could build a very poor story out of so little.

### elliott-rum-003
context: spring, clear afternoon, the beach, 3 hearts
- player: They say Gunther is about to be sacked.
- elliott: That is news to me, and I have no way of knowing it.
- player: You would miss the library.
- elliott: Terribly; I spend my fall and winter afternoons there. I expect the library will outlast the rumor, as libraries usually do.

### elliott-rum-004

### elliott-rum-005

### elliott-rum-017

- player: Does that not bother you?
- elliott: A little, in the vain part of me, which is most of me. Then I remember I am the odd fellow in the shack by the sea, and I go back to work.
